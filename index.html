Sumit Khajuria

A lover of Dogri language, folk culture and theatre. Through his debut book खड़ खक्खड़ (Khad Khakhad), he aims to bring the richness of Dogri storytelling to every reader and preserve it for generations.

Sumit Khajuria is a well-known performer, musician, and author from the Jammu region. He creates popular Dogri music including "Jammu Tawi" and is an All India Radio approved Dogri Folk Artist. He has performed in numerous events organized by Jammu Tourism and the Information & PR Department, Government of J&K. His work as a sarangi player and Dogra poet has made him a prominent voice in Dogri folk culture.

— Sumit Khajuria (Jammu Writer, Dogra Poet & Dogri Musician)
